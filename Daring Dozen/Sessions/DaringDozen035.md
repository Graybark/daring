The party continues investigating the ruined town and meet Reidoth who is willing to guide them to  
the Wave Echo Cave if they chase away or defeat the dragon. As they continue investigating, they  
are surprised by two phase spiders whom they dispatch easily. As they continue discussing their next  
course of action, they attract the attention of the green dragon with whom they have a brief  
conversation. After the talk goes sour, the dragon attacks and Brad, Dio, and Eed are rendered  
unconscious after a devastating dragon breath attack.
