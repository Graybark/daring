# Daring Dozen — Session 73

**In-game date:** 4 Nightal morning to midday (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Jam as Green

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

The party fights two nabassu who ambush them and continue their travels through the Underdark  
after they dispatch them. When the party notices a faint tremor that affects the whole cave system  
surrounding them, they are surprised by a group of five drow who propose a temporary alliance to  
fend of what is likely an approaching neothelid. Cornelius and the others convince their leader Soloj  
Claddar to show them an illusion of the temple of Vanrakdoom and to give them a detailed  
description of the path to the temple. Since Soloj is a warlock, he also shares some resources with  
Cornelius and Green. 4th of Nightal, midday.
