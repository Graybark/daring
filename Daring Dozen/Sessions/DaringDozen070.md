# Daring Dozen — Session 70

**In-game date:** 2 Nightal early morning to midday (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Mahmoud as Klyde

### Absent Players

The party gets tattoos and after that they reconvene at the bookstore and manage to enter the  
room underneath the basement. They investigate the mirror and discover that it is a divine tool that  
sucks people’s memories and is made by Shar. Cornelius sacrifices his memories to the memory to  
gain more power. His only personal memories are some fragments of his husband. This is where  
they meet Klyde who pops out of the mirror and is immediately followed by a group of Sharran  
cultists. They defeat them and preserve one of their heads in Yalda’s lamp. Their leader and another  
escape back through the mirror. 2nd of Nightal, midday.
