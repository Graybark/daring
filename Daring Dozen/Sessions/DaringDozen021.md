# Daring Dozen — Session 21

**In-game date:** 4 Marpenoth middle of the night to 5 Marpenoth morning (end estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Rachel as Aersius

### Absent Players

The party fights off the zombies and then decides to camp an hour outside of Conyberry. While  
resting on a bluff, Aersius notices something bothering the horses and proceeds to investigate. They  
are attacked and almost killed by stirges who have been feeding on the horses. The party comes to  
the rescue and the group decides to take another long rest up top.
