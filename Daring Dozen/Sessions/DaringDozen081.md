7th of Nightal, midday (2024.8.30)  
The party find themselves deep within the mines waiting for the mindflayer patrol to pass them by.  
Yalda: the mind flayer’s soul within the ring is able to cast detect thoughts, dominate monster on  
Yalda. It can also plane shift the ring to save it from destruction or a similar fate. Its goal is to  
influence the party to further his goal of immortality, it is careful not to antagonize Yalda, however.  
Cornelius: CON save DC 25: A failed saving throw will make his progression faster. He may also  
willingly fail. Shar starts to tempt him with more power.  
- Green: can make an arcana check DC 20: the fungus seems familiar in its alien nature  
The party prepares the assault on the mind flayer colony. They encountered four githyanki who  
ordered them to lay down their arms. When negotiations fail, Bridds casts Mass Suggestion on them  
and suggests that they should work together.
