4th of Nightal, midday (2024.6.14)  
The party is preparing for the encounter with the neothelid that is slowly but surely approaching  
their position. As they do so, the following things happen:  
Cornelius  
CON saving throw DC 25: Cornelius notices that he is no longer hungry. A failed saving throw will  
make his progression faster. He may also willingly fail.  
Woodruff  
WIS saving throw DC 20: She notices small white, almost translucent veins growing over her body,  
starting at her waist. If successful, she recognizes these to be foreign to her body. Otherwise, she  
accepts them as being part of everything here, including her. If they are left to grow, she will start  
perceiving thoughts not of her own.  
The tremors become increasingly stronger and when the worm seems to be very close to them, they  
suddenly stop. The duergar make stealth check and depending either approach or attack from the  
hole that seems to have been dug by a purple worm through the ceiling of the cave. Characters with  
high perception hear very faint thuds some distance from the players. The duergar levitate down  
while invisible and enlarged.  
The Mind Flayers  
Zzara'kath is a tall and slender mind flayer with a purple-blue hue to its skin, making it look almost  
ethereal. Its long, slender tentacles writhe constantly, giving an unsettling air of menace. Its eyes  
glow with an eerie green light, which often betrays its calculating and cold nature. Driven by an  
insatiable hunger for knowledge and power, Zzara'kath is the mastermind behind the colossal  
neothelid. It dresses in flowing, dark robes adorned with arcane symbols. Zzara'kath projects  
thoughts that are soft but command attention, reflecting its detached superiority. Zzara'kath regards  
the duergar as useful but expendable tools, valuing their loyalty and endurance but showing no  
concern for their well-being.  
Ulthozz is shorter and bulkier than its counterpart, with dark purple skin that adds to its intimidating  
presence. Its tentacles are shorter and thicker, twitching with barely contained energy. Its eyes are a  
deep, unsettling black, reflecting its unpredictable and volatile nature. Ulthozz revels in chaos and  
destruction, serving as the duo's enforcer. It wears a mix of arcane-infused armor and robes,  
designed to protect while allowing freedom of movement. Ulthozz's thoughts are booming and filled  
with manic energy, striking fear into those who receive them. Ulthozz sees the duergar as little more  
than living shields and instruments of its chaotic desires, enjoying their suffering as much as their  
utility.  
Duergar Slave Warriors  
The duergar’s leader Druknar Ironmind is stocky and muscular, with a bald head and a thick black  
beard that frames his stern face. His piercing gray eyes are often downcast, carrying a haunted,  
faraway look that reveals the weight of his ancestors' torment. His facial expressions are minimal  
and stoic, rarely showing anything beyond a grim determination. As the leader of the duergar  
warriors, Druknar exudes a quiet authority, his commands issued with a resigned tone that  
underscores his deep melancholy. His demeanor is one of quiet endurance, his movements slow and  
deliberate, reflecting a life of suppressed pain and relentless duty. He views the mind flayers with a  
mix of fear and resigned obedience, recognizing them as his masters and oppressors.  
Thorzal Shadowfist is dark-skinned with a shaved head and a gray beard, adding to his grim  
appearance. His face is often set in a deep frown, with eyes that seem perpetually shadowed by an  
inner darkness. A noticeable scar runs down his left cheek, but it's his sorrowful, distant gaze that  
truly reveals his inner turmoil. Thorzal moves with a heavy, almost sluggish gait, as if the weight of  
his memories drags him down. He rarely speaks, and when he does, his voice is a low, gravelly  


his memories drags him down. He rarely speaks, and when he does, his voice is a low, gravelly  
murmur that hints at the depth of his despair. His demeanor is somber and introspective, often lost  
in thought. Thorzal harbors a deep, unspoken resentment towards the mind flayers, but his fear and  
sense of duty keep him in line.  
Borgath Steelvein is short and stout, with a dark gray beard and piercing blue eyes that seem to see  
through to one's soul. His eyes are often glassy and unfocused, reflecting a deep, unspoken grief. He  
wears a constant expression of quiet sorrow, with the corners of his mouth turned down as if  
weighed by perpetual sadness. Borgath's movements are slow and deliberate, his shoulders  
perpetually hunched as if bearing an invisible burden. When he speaks, his voice carries a tone of  
melancholy, each word tinged with regret. His demeanor is one of stoic resignation, often lost in  
thoughts of what once was and what might have been. Borgath views the mind flayers with a sense  
of fatalistic acceptance, believing that rebellion is futile against such powerful beings.  
Kromdul Ironhide is muscular with a braided white beard and tattoos covering his arms, each one a  
story of battle and survival. His remaining eye is a dull, lifeless gray, often staring off into the  
distance as if haunted by unseen horrors. His face is frequently set in a scowl, but beneath the anger  
lies a deep-seated melancholy. Kromdul's behavior is erratic, swinging between aggressive outbursts  
and moments of silent brooding. He often isolates himself from others, retreating into his own  
thoughts. His voice, when he speaks, is harsh and filled with a bitterness that hints at long-buried  
pain. His demeanor is volatile, with an undercurrent of profound sadness that seeps into every  
action. Kromdul views the mind flayers with a mix of hatred and fear, his aggressive nature barely  
kept in check by the knowledge of their overwhelming power.  
The Drow  
Soloj Claddar is a cunning and ambitious drow with striking white hair styled in intricate braids  
adorned with small spider-shaped charms. His piercing bright red eyes hint at his dark connections,  
often staring intensely as if listening to unheard whispers. Soloj's face is frequently set in a  
determined, almost fanatical expression. He commands both fear and respect, with a voice that  
carries authority and a demeanor that brooks no dissent.  
Vellin Abuin is a skilled and ruthless drow with long silver hair usually tied back in a practical yet  
elegant ponytail. Her rare rose-hued eyes add an unusual and striking feature to her appearance,  
often narrowing in suspicion or calculation. She prefers to operate in the shadows, her presence  
almost ghostly as she blends with her surroundings. Vellin's voice is sharp and cutting, often used to  
relay tactical commands or deliver biting sarcasm. While her exterior is cool and detached, those  
who know her understand that her loyalty is fierce and unwavering, especially to her comrades.  
Filfaere Cormrael is an enchanting and powerful sorceress with dark skin and silver eyes that seem  
to hold ancient mysteries. Her long, flowing pale blonde hair cascades down her back in soft waves.  
Filfaere's face often bears a calm expression, masking the fierce determination within. Filfaere's  
voice is melodic and soothing, often used to chant incantations or offer sage advice. Beneath her  
tranquil exterior lies a sorceress deeply connected to the arcane energies of the Underdark.  
Viedrar Olabban is a formidable drow fighter with a tall and muscular build, his short, neatly kept  
hair adding to his soldier-like appearance. His most distinctive feature is his prosthetic arm, which  
shimmers with a faint blue light, hinting at its magical properties. Viedrar's amber eyes are rare and  
striking, his face usually set in a stoic expression. Viedrar's voice is deep and commanding, used to  
relay battle tactics or maintain order. Viedrar is deeply loyal to his comrades, his tactical prowess  
and strength making him a crucial member of the group.  
Thalra Sylnar is an agile and perceptive drow with shoulder-length copper hair often tied back into a  
simple, practical bun. Her bright green eyes often scan the surroundings with a keen gaze. Thalra's  
face bears a focused expression, reflecting her sharp instincts and exceptional tracking abilities.  
Thalra's voice is soft and measured, used sparingly to offer guidance or relay information. Beneath  
her quiet exterior lies a fierce warrior, always ready to protect her companions and strike from the  
shadows with deadly precision.
