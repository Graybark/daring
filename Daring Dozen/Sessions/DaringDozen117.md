### Summary  

The party stealthily infiltrate the Talos hideout and with the help of Yalda's father, they find the  
secret entrance to Ularan's lair. They have to dive into a remote lake and enter a very deep passage  
to surface inside it. On the way, they have to endure a prolonged phase without oxygen and Klyde  
unfortunately does not make it through and suffocates just before the fight with the lich begins.  
They battle the lich who has cast a Globe of Invulnerability on himself and is enclosed in a protective  
circle. The party struggle.
