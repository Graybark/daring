### Summary  

The party decides to recruit the cultists to gain some advantage on the lich. They manage to  
convince everyone that Yalda is Talos' chosen and that she is here to set Fheralai straight. After  
putting on a show, they convince her and her bodyguards to join the fight. They teleport inside the  
cave and manage to defeat the lich quite easily since he was still busy restoring the circle. They help  
Fheralai and her guard to escape the lair and decide to teleport to the Shard of the Moon in  
Neverwinter.
