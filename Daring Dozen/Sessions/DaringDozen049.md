# Daring Dozen — Session 49

**In-game date:** 13 Uktar evening to 14 Uktar morning (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Jam as Kerri

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

The party defeats the elementals and save the fishermen and soldiers. They proceed towards the  
temple of the Dawnfather where they find the missing children. They find some diamond dust as  
well and bring it to Merrygold who heals Aersius, who in turn decides to leave the party as  
adventuring with them is too dangerous.  
Cornelius discovers more about the Nightwalker who seems to need some sacrifice to trade places  
with it to enter the Material Plane from the Negative Plane. Kerri considers the possibility that it  
might have some connection with the wizard’s dead husband who did do some unknown research.  
While sleeping, Eed wanders off and is not seen again. Kerri notices her trance-like states as she  
leaves into the night.  
The party takes a long rest and visit a store with oddities owned by an Illuskan man named Aubrey  
Silverspurn who tells Dio that he gets a lot of his wares from merchants who have actually missed  
their expected return date. He does not know much about their trade deals and contracts in general  
though. 14th of Utkar, morning.
