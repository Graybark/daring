### Summary  

The party prepares for the mission in the Mere of Dead Men. They are provided 3 elite soldiers, some basic  
supplies and a phial blessed by Selune herself. Since Cornelius is out of spell slots, they decide to rest for the day  
and start their mission the next morning. Yalda prays to Talos and has a vision of returning home to the sunken  
city beyond the coast. While the party discuss their plans, they notice people entering the general's headquarters  
and among them a woman who is inclined to flirt with Yalda. The woman introduces herself as Adria and after  
some flirting she invites Yalda on a walk. Swift is asked by Cornelous to follow and observe them. After Swift is  
satisfied that everything is safe, she returns to the party. After a while, Yalda is invited to Adria's townhouse and  
when there she tries to charm Yalda magically but when that fails she attacks Yalda and thereby reveals her  
vampire form. Yalda is barely able to fight off the vampire, but the rest of the party is surrounded by ten other  
vampires and attacked. Since Swift pulled out the Tear of Selune, the vampires make devastating use of their  
Command ability and manage to overwhelm the party. Yalda is still minutes away from them.  

### Dramatis Personae  

Ser Caldrin Veyr: A broad-shouldered human in his late 40s with close-cropped salt-and-pepper hair  
and a scar running from his temple to his jaw. His plate armor is well-maintained but shows dents and  
repairs from years of use. Backstory: Once a decorated officer of a border legion, Caldrin resigned  
after a disastrous campaign where politics cost him half his men. Now he sells his expertise only to  
causes he deems worthy, drilling allies into disciplined units and leading from the front.  
Nyssa Kael: A wiry half-elf woman with pale skin, cropped black hair, and quick, restless eyes. She  
favors dark leather and keeps her weapons minimal and practical. Backstory: Taken in young by a  
thieves’ guild, Nyssa learned silence and patience as survival. She broke from her guild after a  
betrayal and now works on her own terms. She prefers contracts where her skills can dismantle  
corrupt leaders rather than serve them.  
Orven Dhal: A gaunt tiefling with crimson skin, curling horns polished to a shine, and ink-stained  
hands. His robes are patched with fire-scorched holes, and he carries a staff marked with etched  
runes. Backstory: Orven was once a scholar at an arcane college, expelled after a forbidden  
experiment nearly destroyed part of the campus and permanently damaged his lungs, rendering him  
mute. He has since embraced the battlefield as his laboratory, honing destructive magic with ruthless  
precision.
