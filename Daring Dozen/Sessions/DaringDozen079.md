6th of Nightal, midday (2024.8.16)  
The party find themselves deep within the mines of the Duergar village waiting for the mindflayer  
patrol to pass them by.  
Yalda: the mind flayer’s soul within the ring is able to cast detect thoughts, dominate monster on  
Yalda. It can also plane shift the ring to save it from destruction or a similar fate. Its goal is to  
influence the party to further his goal of immortality, it is careful not to antagonize Yalda, however.  
Cornelius: CON save DC 25: A failed saving throw will make his progression faster. He may also  
willingly fail. Shar starts to tempt him with more power.  
- Green: can make an arcana check DC 20: the fungus seems familiar in its alien nature  
While waiting for the mind flayer patrol to leave, Woodruff revives Green who gets incarnated as a  
human with brown hair and of short stature. He is seemingly of sound mind and remembers nothing  
while he was “Green”. His name is Bridd~s and he used to be a triton scientist who discovered  
something he should not have deep within the ocean depths.  
After another hour has passed, Morgra, the chief miner of the duergar village, picks them up and  
leads them back to the village. The mind flayers seem to not have noticed anything unusual in the  
village. They are led to Gritta’s tavern where most of them get drunk and Klyde even starts a brawl  
with Morgra, although he loses.  
Sober Cornelius strikes a deal with Thalgrim where the latter promised the Dying Dozen the  
duergar’s aid against the sharrans if they help them against the mind flayers. Eventually the party  
goes to sleep, many of them drunk. Session ends 6th of Nightal, late evening.  
The mind flayer Zzara'kath is a tall and slender mind flayer with a purple-blue hue to its skin, making  
it look almost ethereal. Its long, slender tentacles writhe constantly, giving an unsettling air of  
menace. Its eyes glow with an eerie green light, which often betrays its calculating and cold nature.  
Driven by an insatiable hunger for knowledge and power, Zzara'kath is the mastermind behind the  
colossal neothelid. It dresses in flowing, dark robes adorned with arcane symbols. Zzara'kath  
projects thoughts that are soft but command attention, reflecting its detached superiority.  
Zzara'kath regards the duergar as useful but expendable tools, valuing their loyalty and endurance  
but showing no concern for their well-being.  
QUALITH DOOR LOCKS  
Mounted on both sides of every door in the mind flayer colony (areas 9 through 20) is a burnished  
plate of dark, alien metal embossed with a braille-like form of tactile writing known as Qualith. Mind  
flayers use their tentacles to read Qualith, which is composed in four-line stanzas indecipherable to  
other creatures. A creature that touches a Qualith inscription, however, can receive fragmentary  
insight into the multilayered thoughts contained in it. A non-illithid that wants to understand a  
Qualith inscription in Seadeeps can make a DC 18 Intelligence check to interpret the inscription. A  
failed attempt results in a crushing headache and requires the creature to succeed on a DC 11  
Wisdom saving throw or develop a random form of short-term madness (see “Madness” in chapter 8  
of the Dungeon Master’s Guide). A comprehend languages spell provides understanding of the  
inscription roughly equivalent to what a mind flayer would get from it.  
Each door plate is imbued with psionic energy that functions like an arcane lock spell. The Qualith  
inscription on the plate describes the genuflections a mind flayer must make with its tentacles to  
open the door. Other creatures with at least four tentacles (such as flumphs) can imitate these  
motions to bypass the lock, provided they can interpret the inscription. A creature must be within 5  
feet of the door to open it in this way. Creatures without the requisite tentacles and knowledge  
must deal with the lock by more conventional means, such as a knock spell. If magic is unavailable, a  
locked door can be forced open with a successful DC 30 Strength (Athletics) check. Clever  
adventurers might discover that a decapitated mind flayer head, animated through an animate dead  
spell, can be used to access the locks.
