# Daring Dozen — Session 8

**In-game date:** 18 Eleint morning to 18 Eleint evening (estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Polina as Eed

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

The party fails to take the castle and is beaten back and chased through the forest. They are  
eventually captured by goblins, but Eed suddenly transforms and scares the goblins to the bone and  
makes them flee. They reflect on what went wrong as they nurse their bruised bodies and souls.  
Maybe they could have succeeded at the rescue mission if they had prevented the goblins to flee  
and had not let the other ones go. Maybe they could have arrived at the castle sooner if they had  
left the horses behind and hurried. Maybe they could have watched the castle and looked for weak  
points. They decide to go back to Phandalin to find better equipment or reinforcements to take the  
castle. Or maybe look for clues on who this Black Spider is that put a bounty on them.
