### Summary

Yalda's clone woke after the Black Spider's procedure succeeded: the illithid's consciousness had been transferred into the new body. The party gave the Black Spider three days to continue his research with the illithid.

During those three days, Yalda learned that her patron had been the Nightwalker. She contacted Talos, who helped her renounce that connection. With the Nightwalker's hold on her severed, Yalda became a full-fledged level 20 sorcerer. She also investigated the Black Spider and found research materials and notes, but no sign of ill intent.

Woodruff sought peace after what she had endured. She tried to reconnect to the network on three nights; the first two attempts failed, and the third succeeded. After several attempts to reach Veylar, she finally heard a brief reply: he forgave her. The exchange brought her some calm. Ithrelle spoke with the Raven Queen, who blessed her and the party for their coming fight against the Nightwalker.

Ithrelle also revealed an incident from her youth. While fighting powerful undead, she survived a battle that killed several fellow elven warriors from her order and left a large scar across her torso. Looking at the scar, Woodruff sensed a connection to the strange events surrounding them. Yalda, Woodruff, and Ithrelle discussed it in a *Magnificent Mansion*, which also gave Woodruff space to seek peace and Yalda space to reckon with the two father figures in her life.

The party prepared for the confrontation. They learned spells and used *Heroes' Feast*, *Death Ward*, air bubbles, *Simulacrum*, *Mind Blank*, and *Foresight*. They also created clones and placed them in an interdimensional space about 30 meters on each side, where the bodies could develop. As a favor to the Black Spider, they made a clone of him with a ninth-level spell. Cornelius acquired a powerful staff. They created a special tuning fork using the curse Yalda was born with; the notes do not specify its properties.

Once the transport ritual was ready, the party returned to the Black Spider. Although Yalda's link to the Nightwalker had been cut, some of that connection appeared to remain in her clone. The Black Spider used it to locate the Nightwalker in his plane. The party then plane-shifted and teleported into a complete void. At their destination, the only visible object was a large steel sphere. The session ended there.
