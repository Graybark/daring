### Summary

The party travelled through the Negative Energy Plane toward an enormous metal sphere, roughly two kilometres across. *Death Ward* helped protect them, but the journey still required repeated saving throws. Before they entered the sphere, a beam scanned Yalda and she vanished. Magical contact established that she was being held in a cell.

The others found a possible entrance and began blasting it, hearing mechanical noises from within. A talking skeleton acting as a border official intervened, and the party was brought inside. They learned that the sphere contained a city: a vessel said to have travelled from another dimension, now inhabited by liches, vampires, and other undead.

The official seemed to be withholding information about the Nightwalker. A party member successfully used *Modify Memory* to make him believe that his ruler expected the visitors, wanted them treated like royalty, and had instructed him to answer their questions. The party learned that Yalda had been taken by a lich called the Listener.

With a skeletal escort, they set off to meet the city's horned ruler, hoping he would order Yalda's release. On the way, a vampire feeding on a goblin stopped them. It attacked when they spoke to it. The party fought it off rather than killing it, and the session ended after that confrontation.
