# Daring Dozen — Session 28

**In-game date:** 29 Marpenoth midday to 30 Marpenoth evening (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Rachel as Aersius
- Mahmoud as Dio
- Polina as Eed
- Jam as Bradwick

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

The party returns to Phandalin and performs an epic retelling of their quest at the inn. While Dio and  
Brad are busy doing that, Eed and Aersius notice two suspicious characters watching them and  
shortly after leaving the inn. Aersius and Eed pursue and manage to follow them back to the woods  
next to the Tresendar manor. Cornelius collects Brad and Dio and the three follow Eed and Aersius  
and get ambushed by a group of humans of which one was the woman who was present at the inn.  
Aersius and Eed follow the other suspicious character, a heavily-clothed man with almost claw-like  
hands, and notice him entering the cave system underneath the manor. When Cornelius casts a  
fireball into the attacking human group, the clawed man returns with company and a three-sided  
battle ensues.  
30th of Marpenoth, evening
