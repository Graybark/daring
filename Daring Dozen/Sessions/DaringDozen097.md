### Summary  

The party returns to Neverwinter and Cedric's mansion where they show Cedric the evidence they  
gathered and relate what they have seen there. Cedric is convinced and will try to talk to his sister  
Halia Thornton. Before that, Woodruff cures herself, Klyde, and Swift from the affliction caused by  
the fungal spores using Greater Restoration. This also erases the memory that Veylar implanted into  
her mind. She mentions this to him when they talk to Cedric and he restores the memory according  
to her wishes.  
Bridds is restored to life as a triton with only his finger and pain in the chest retaining evidence of  
any outside influence. He faces an existential crisis when he realizes that he is not entirely free.  
Though he has a clear mind, he seems to forget knowledge related to his studies of nature. Bridds  
discovers that the sword "given" to him by a solar is an ancient, sentient weapon named Thalamun.  
They also talked to Darlan Greaves, commander of the guard, and convince him that he should not  
send more soldiers to a senseless death in the forest. He will try to talk to Dagult Neverember and  
convince him to stop sending people there.  
Veylar and "Ceddi", Cedric's clone, will meanwhile try to talk to Halia Thornton.
