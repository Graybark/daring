# Daring Dozen — Session 2

**In-game date:** 14 Eleint afternoon to 15 Eleint morning (estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Emma as Friyola

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

The party leaves Cragmaw Hideout and accompanies Sildar Hallwinter back to Phandalin. On the way  
they encounter twig blights being chased by a fire elemental but do not intervene in the hunt. When  
they arrive in Phandalin, they spend a night at Stonehill Inn after which Sildar leaves them to arrange  
the reward of 50 gold he promised the party. Friyola does not trust Sildar, however, and convinces  
him to leave his sword in case he wants to run away without paying the party.
