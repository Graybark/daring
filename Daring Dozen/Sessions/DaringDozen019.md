# Daring Dozen — Session 19

**In-game date:** 3 Marpenoth early morning to morning (estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Polina as Eed

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

• Wyvern Tor (optional): The party has been given the task by Harbin Wester, the  
Townmaster, to drive away some orcs that have been camping and raiding at Wyvern Tor. They also  
know that the Many-Arrows orcs that they met 10 days ago (18th of Eleint) on their way to Cragmaw  
Castle were looking for those orcs for some reason.  
• Agatha’s Lair: Sister Garaele would like the party to seek out Agatha the witch to ask her  
about the location of Bowgentle’s spellbook. Sister Garaele advises the party to treat Agatha with  
utmost respect and gives the party a silver comb as a gift for Agatha. Her home is close to  
Conyberry.  
• Thundertree Ruins (druid Reidoth): Qeline Alderleaf of Alderleaf Farm told Bradwick of  
Reidoth the druid who is very knowledgeable about the area and might help the party find what  
they are looking for. Reidoth offers to accompany them to Wave Echo Cave if they chase away the  
green dragon.  
• Conyberry/ Old Owl Well (Hamun Kost): Sildar Hallwinter told Cornelius that there have  
been reports of strange happenings in the area around Conyberry. Someone also seems to be  
excavating there.  
The party report back to Sildar and receive leads from him and other NPCs in town. Eed prays to  
Tymora goddess of luck while looking for clues but is visited by her patron the same night. They  
decide to follow up on the Conyberry lead and travel there. On their way they are accosted by three  
manticores who are looking for Hyppolite. The party manage to deceive the monstrosities and  
merely have to sacrifice 3 of their horses to get away unscathed. The party arrive at Conyberry and  
debate whether or not they should enter the courtyard of the tower that dominates the town ruins.  
3rd of Marpenoth, morning.
