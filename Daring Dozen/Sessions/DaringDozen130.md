### Summary

After the Necrofungal Colossus was destroyed, the party traveled to Neverwinter. A guard with a connection to someone they knew let them through the gate. Yalda suggested finding magical equipment for Woodruff. While searching, they noticed a thin tiefling boy of about ten following them. They befriended him, learned he was called Boy, and he led them to a magical item shop.

The shop was run by an elderly woman who did not seem entirely human. Customers pictured an item and made a Wisdom check to find it; more unusual items were harder to locate. Yalda found a staff, and Woodruff replaced some missing equipment. The exact items were not given in the recap.

At the temple of Selûne, the party spoke with Varos and asked an assistant to look after Boy. They discussed where the spores had come from and whether they were connected to Shar, the Nightwalker, or the Far Realm. These remained questions rather than conclusions.

Woodruff cast *Greater Restoration* on Ithrelle's crown. The party removed it and put it in a bag of holding. After a long rest, they prepared to visit the Black Spider.

## Open threads
