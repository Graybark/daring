# Daring Dozen — Session 57

**In-game date:** 18 Uktar morning to 19 Uktar early morning (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Alëna as Woodruff

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

The party meet a druid named Woodruff who also experiences memory loss. This connection is not  
lost on Cornelius who also deals with gaps in his memory. The party rests at the inn and when they  
wake, the inn owner is missing, along with one of the horses. They take the remaining two animals  
and continue towards Leilon and the Mere of Dead Men. On their way there while sleeping inside  
their wizard’s Tiny Hut, they are accosted by a mysterious man who is most likely a vampire who  
wants to “ask Cornelius questions” and wants him to come with him. The confrontation comes to a  
head when the session ends. 19th of Uktar, early morning.
