Veylar’s Scheme: Neverwinter’s Rebuilding Efforts  
The city of Neverwinter is in a delicate phase of recovery. After years of turmoil—ranging from the  
eruption of Mount Hotenow to political upheaval—the city is rebuilding its infrastructure and  
expanding its defenses. This requires vast quantities of lumber, much of which must come from the  
Neverwinter Wood, the sprawling forest that has long stood as a source of mystery and magic.  
The Lord Protector, Dagult Neverember, and his council are grappling with the logistics of balancing  
the city's needs and the growing unrest from local druids, rangers, and citizens who fear the  
ecological consequences. The tension is a perfect opportunity for Veylar to insert himself into the  
heart of Neverwinter’s politics.  
Veylar’s Role and Strategy  
Disguised as Veylar Ashbough, a charismatic and "concerned" druid, Veylar presents himself as a  
mediator who can represent the interests of the forest while helping the city achieve its goals.  
Step 1: Establish Credibility  
Veylar approaches Neverember’s court as a humble yet eloquent figure, supported by a  
growing number of druids and concerned citizens. He offers solutions that sound practical, like  
selective logging, magical regrowth, or alternative trade agreements to reduce the burden on  
the forest. His proposals are just plausible enough to gain the council’s attention while subtly  
entrenching him in their discussions.  
1.  
Key Ploys:  
Charm and Diplomacy: Veylar appeals to the moral compass of the decision-  
makers, positioning himself as an ally of "compromise."  
o  
Sabotage Alternative Solutions: He discreetly orchestrates the failure of other  
sustainable proposals (e.g., sabotaging trade caravans or making selective logging  
efforts fail via forest creatures under his control).  
o  
Foster Public Unrest: Through proxies, he fans the flames of protests against the  
logging efforts, creating a need for a "moderate voice"—himself.  
o  
Step 2: Divide and Weaken  
As Veylar becomes involved in the decision-making process, he sows distrust among the  
council. He encourages competing factions within the city, such as merchants, nobles, and  
environmentalists, to turn on one another. Each faction believes they are gaining his favor  
while unknowingly playing into his larger plan.  
2.  
Key Ploys:  
Merchant Sabotage: Veylar secretly backs certain merchant guilds, ensuring they  
get preferential access to the lumber trade. This creates resentment among rival  
guilds and weakens their unity.  
o  
Rally the Common Folk: Through hired agitators and charm, Veylar positions  
himself as a hero to the people, using the growing protests to pressure  
Neverember into granting him greater influence.  
o  
Step 3: The Hidden Agenda  
While Veylar presents himself as the forest’s defender, his true goal is to manipulate  
Neverwinter into granting him significant control over the Neverwinter Wood. Once he  
secures this authority, he will slowly transform the forest into a dangerous, enchanted domain  
under his total rule. He envisions turning the entire forest into a living weapon—a poisoned,  
magical barrier that keeps Neverwinter under his thumb.

How the Party Gets Involved  
First Contact:  
The party encounters Veylar Ashbough during a protest near Neverwinter’s gates. He is  
mediating between city guards and angry locals, presenting himself as a voice of reason. If the  
party sides with the protesters or shows concern for the forest, Veylar takes an immediate  
interest in them, offering to hire them to investigate "unsanctioned logging operations" in the  
woods.  
1.  
Objective: Investigate and shut down an illegal lumber camp (which Veylar secretly orchestrated).  
Unraveling the Truth:  
As the party works with Veylar, they notice inconsistencies:  
The illegal logging camps are oddly well-funded, with signs of sabotage designed  
to escalate tensions.  
o  
Veylar seems to have an uncanny understanding of the city’s internal politics and  
future plans.  
o  
Wildlife in the forest exhibits strange behavior, seemingly targeting certain  
factions while sparing others.  
o  
2.  
Objective: The party must uncover Veylar's hidden connections and motivations before he gains  
further influence.  
The Turning Point:  
When the party gathers enough evidence or disrupts one of Veylar’s schemes, he shifts tactics.  
He uses his growing public favor to brand the party as instigators of unrest or as enemies of  
Neverwinter’s progress. This forces the party to decide: expose him to the city at great  
personal risk, or retreat and regroup for a final confrontation.  
3.  
Potential Outcomes  
If Veylar Wins:  
The dragon solidifies his control over Neverwinter Wood, transforming it into a corrupted  
haven that gives him immense power. Neverwinter itself becomes a pawn in his greater game,  
and the region slowly falls under his shadow.  
If the Party Prevails:  
Exposing Veylar destabilizes the city's politics but spares the forest. However, Veylar retreats,  
plotting revenge and forcing the party to prepare for his inevitable return.  
Adjusted Setup: Party Resting in Neverwinter Wood  
The party’s presence in Neverwinter Wood does not go unnoticed. While resting, they encounter  
strange happenings in the forest—wild animals behaving aggressively, a sense of unease among the  
trees, and whispers of a mysterious druid rallying local factions. Veylar Ashbough seizes this  
opportunity to approach the party directly, leveraging their position to aid his machinations.  
He arrives at their camp under the pretense of seeking allies against Neverwinter’s reckless logging.  
Veylar suggests the party investigate a nearby illegal logging camp, positioning himself as their  
trusted ally while subtly tying them into his broader scheme to destabilize Neverwinter.

Encounter: Veylar Ashbough’s First Meeting with the Party  
Setting:  
The party is resting in a serene clearing deep within Neverwinter Wood. The canopy overhead filters  
sunlight into a patchwork of gold and green, but an eerie silence blankets the area. Unseen eyes  
watch from the shadows as the party goes about their day.  

Introduction of Veylar Ashbough  
The first sign of Veylar’s approach is a faint rustling in the trees. A soft voice calls out, calm and  
nonthreatening:  
“Travelers, I mean you no harm. May I step into the light?”  
Moments later, a tall human man emerges from the foliage. His robes are forest-green, and a silver  
oak-leaf pendant hangs at his chest. His emerald eyes gleam with a mix of warmth and intelligence.  
A friendly smile spreads across his face as he raises his hands in a gesture of peace.  
Veylar’s Dialogue:  
“Forgive my intrusion. It is rare to find kindred spirits in these woods, especially ones who seem so...  
capable. I am Veylar Ashbough, a humble servant of the forest.”  
The Offer  
Veylar opens with pleasantries, expressing admiration for the party’s apparent strength and  
initiative. He quickly pivots to the conflict between Neverwinter and the forest, presenting himself as  
a mediator seeking balance between the city’s expansion and the forest’s preservation.  
Key Points Veylar Makes:  
On Logging: “Neverwinter’s ambitions grow unchecked, and the woods pay the price.  
Creatures grow restless, ancient spirits are disturbed, and yet the city sees only timber and  
coin.”  
On His Role: “I have worked tirelessly to broker a compromise—limited logging, replanting  
efforts—but greed clouds their judgment. I fear this balance may shatter without... outside  
intervention.”  
The Mission: Veylar claims that an illegal logging camp is operating deep in the woods, taking  
more than even Neverwinter’s officials have sanctioned. He requests the party’s help in  
shutting it down, saying, “A show of force will send a message that these lands are not  
unguarded.”  
Clues to His True Nature  
Though Veylar’s charm and eloquence make him convincing, perceptive characters may notice  
subtle inconsistencies:  
Animal Reactions: The party’s mounts or nearby forest creatures may act unnerved by his  
presence. A successful Insight (DC 15) reveals a sense of unease in the wildlife.  
1.  
Strange Coincidences: If asked about the illegal logging camp, his answers seem rehearsed,  
and his knowledge of specific details might feel too precise for someone who claims to “just  
have heard rumors.”  
2.  
Arcane Energy: A character who casts Detect Magic will notice faint enchantment magic  
emanating from Veylar’s pendant, which he claims is “a druidic heirloom.”  
3.  
What Happens Next?  
If the party accepts his request:  
Veylar gives them directions to a small logging operation. The site is indeed illicit, but it has  
been set up by Veylar himself through proxies like Therren Hallowbranch to escalate tensions.  
Veylar secretly sends a group of his mercenaries to watch the party, ensuring they complete  
the task but also gauging their strength.  
He praises their efforts afterward, deepening his "ally" persona while subtly planting seeds of  
mistrust about Neverwinter's leadership.  
If the party is suspicious or refuses:  
Veylar feigns disappointment but remains cordial, saying, “I understand. These matters are not  
for everyone.” He disappears into the woods, but they will later encounter signs of his  
interference, such as wildlife acting strangely or villagers blaming the party for forest troubles.
