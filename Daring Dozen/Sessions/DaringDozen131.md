### Summary

After resting in Neverwinter, the party teleported to the Black Spider's cave. Yalda learned that he had used her blood to create an unconscious duplicate of her. He intended to transfer the illithid Zzara'kath's soul from Yalda's ring into that body, hoping to obtain information the illithid possessed.

The Black Spider said his research would let him pinpoint the Nightwalker in its plane. He claimed the planes were merging and interfering with magic. He also paid the party 5,000 gold he owed them. The party explored a difficult-to-reach area of the cave and sensed powerful magical energy without identifying its source. They learned they would need air and *Death Ward* to survive travel to the Negative Energy Plane.

The session ended when the Black Spider completed the transfer ritual and Yalda's duplicate opened its eyes. The available recap did not yet prove that the transfer had succeeded. Session 132 later established that the procedure worked.
