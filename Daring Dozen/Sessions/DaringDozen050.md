# Daring Dozen — Session 50

**In-game date:** 14 Uktar morning to late morning (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Hasti as Yalda
- Mahmoud as Dio
- Jam as Kerri

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

While Dio and Cornelius shop and gather information, Kerri meets two strangers, both half-elves  
who are also enquiring into the strange weather patterns. After some negotiation they decide to  
climb the House of Thalivar and encounter 3 soldiers on their way up who seem concerned and  
move away from the tower. Yalda panics after being questioned about her magic fog cloud and  
attacks one of the guards. The party sees no choice but to dispatch the guards and hide their bodies  
inside Swift’s Bag of Holding. They enter the tower after unlocking its gates via the Knock spell. 14th  
of Uktar, late morning.
