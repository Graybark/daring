### Summary  

They arrive at the Sleeping Giant Inn to sleep. A new guest character Bergamont was introduced  
who is an assassin looking for Swift for unknown reasons. They were looking for pliers to use them to  
pull out Swift's teeth with them. She convinced Bridds to take him up to their room when everyone  
else except for Veylar was also having drinks and talks downstairs. Yalda told Swift and the drunk  
Woodruff about her commune where she tried to contact the source of her power and was looking  
for answers to her longing and questions. Unfortunately, she got noticed by something else that  
tried to control her instead. She resisted the foreign power in the end. After this, the lantern started  
to change and Yalda's origin poem started to appear on it.  
Upstairs, Bridds and Bergamont encountered Veylar who promptply cast the Dominate spell on the  
stranger because she seemed suspicious. After some tense discussion, everyone relaxed and tried to  
talk this out which is when they were attacked by a drow who first shot a crossbow bolt through the  
window to hit Swift and then crashed into the room to attack them. After he was defenestrated by  
Klyde, the party, notably Bridds, Klyde, and Yalda, pursused him out into the street.
