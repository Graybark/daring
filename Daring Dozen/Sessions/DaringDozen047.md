# Daring Dozen — Session 47

**In-game date:** 13 Uktar morning to evening (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Absent Players

The party meets 4 officers of the Neverwinter guard on their way to Leilon and are also ambushed  
by an Invisible Stalker. When they reach the town, they encounter about 50 people milling about  
and shouting over each other, arguing. They were told that the town was attacked and is unsafe.  
Merrygold tasked them to save two children and the fishermen and that she would be able to  
restore Aersius to life if they brought her a diamond from the temple within the town. Just when  
they discuss their next course of action, they are attacked by humanoid riders on water horses. 13th  
of Uktar, evening.
