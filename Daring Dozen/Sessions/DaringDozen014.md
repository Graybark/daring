Highharvestide: Preparations are being made for the celebrations. Many-colored triangle flags are  
being hung on buildings across the main streets. Small stalls and tables are being setup.  
Once set up:  
• Tavern brawl  
• Arm wrestling contest  
• Unique beer (cherry or ginger)  
• Extremely hot chili (con save DC 10)  
Miner’s Exchange: As you approach the Miner’s Exchange that has been pointed out to you by Bren,  
you notice a smaller building next to it that in contrast to most of the other buildings is of a more  
elaborate construction design than of the simple and cheap log cabin style. The actual Miner’s  
Exchange appears to be the tallest building in town (3-storied) and though less intricate than the  
small house next to it, it stands out with its rich brown wood and white brick construction. Once  
inside, you are greeted by an older gentleman (gray hair, receding hairline, average height, vest, gray  
clothes). There are several other employees (gnomish woman with a pixie cut, half-elf with  
ambiguous features). The first room is filled with small desks, paper stacks, scrolls, various tool such  
as compasses and abacuses. The older man leads you to Halia Thornton’s office adjacent to the main  
room on the first floor. There’s a big desk with neatly organized stacks of paper and writing utensils.  
Behind a dark desk sits Halia Thornton.  
“Welcome to the Miner’s Exchange. Unfortunately, I can’t offer seats to all of you but make  
yourselves as comfortable as possible.”  
Session: The meeting with Halia does not go very well because Dio tries to sneak a peek at a  
document on Halia’s desk. She kicks them out and further attempts by Cornelius to smooth things  
over are not successful.  
So, the party decides to take care of the Redbrand gang that has been causing trouble in Phandalin.  
They manage to defeat a few Redbrands and bugbears, but Glasstaff eludes their grasp. There are  
hints of him having been present though. 28th of Elein, early afternoon.
