# Daring Dozen — Session 46

**In-game date:** 12 Uktar evening to 13 Uktar morning (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Mahmoud as Dio
- Jam as Kerri (Crack)

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

The party is surrounded by 3 hounds of darkness that let out a terrifying howl and frighten the party.  
A fight ensues that the hounds seem to win but then Dio turns the tides by casting Hypnotic Gaze  
successfully on all the hounds. When one of the hounds finally gets free, it grabs Cornelius and tries  
to escape with him but is forced to let him go when its life is threatened. Just before Cornelius deals  
the deathblow to it, the hound’s face transforms into a featureless visage that says, “Don’t resist.  
Come to me.” After the hounds are dispatched, Cornelius and Crack discuss the possible reasons for  
the hounds chasing the former and try to figure out the connection with his husband. Cornelius does  
remember the featureless visage during the funeral as the face of his husband but determines that  
this was a false memory planted by the Nightwalker. Sometime later they are visited by two bards  
who seek safety in numbers along the journey (Silla Scalesweep and Tarbin Tul). They have some  
reservations towards them but decide to share their magical hut with them, nevertheless. On their  
night watch, they hear and see a giant dark form searching above their heads. They decide to not  
take any action and keep quiet to not alert the dark form. Expenses paid. 13th of Uktar, morning.
