As Bridd~s reels from the first strike, the clearing shifts. The false trees no longer pretend. Their bark  
splits into fleshy ridges, limbs unfurl into jagged, root-wrapped claws, and a massive form—the true  
center of the network—rises from the earth. A violet-hued horror of rotting bark, tangled limbs, and  
twitching body parts, made up of all manner of creatures.  
Suddenly, Woodruff stiffens mid-step. Her eyes glaze over, pupils swallowed by a pale violet  
shimmer. Vines surge from the soil at her feet, wrapping around her legs and yanking her into the  
writhing mass of the hivemind. Cornelius lets out a choked sound as something tendril-thin and  
sharp lashes around his ankle, pulling him from the edge of the glade with unnatural speed. Yalda  
tries to cry out, but her voice is swallowed by a choking wave of spores as she too is pulled under,  
her limbs locking up mid-resistance.  
The hivemind absorbs them, folding their bodies into its twitching bulk. You see their forms inside  
it—barely visible beneath shifting layers of violet flesh and necrotic bark. Their eyes move. Their  
chests rise. They're alive… but bound. Restrained. Soon to be part of the network.  
And the clearing is no longer still. Three more necrohulks rise, stepping forward in unison as the air  
ripples with the same pulsing rhythm that echoes from within the hivemind.  
Initiative continues—with three of your own now trapped inside the enemy.  

### Summary  

The party fights the hivemind and the hulks and fairly quickly everyone but Swift is subdued and  
grafted to the hulks. In a feat of desperation, Swift manages to inflicht sufficient damage to the  
hivemind for it to expell Cornelius, Woodruff, and Yalda who immediately join the fight and heal  
their companions. The fight is not as desperate anymore and seems manageable though difficult.  
They are now trapped within the clearing that is now dim light.
