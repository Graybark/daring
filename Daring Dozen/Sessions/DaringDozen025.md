# Daring Dozen — Session 25

**In-game date:** 25 Marpenoth afternoon to 26 Marpenoth morning (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Rachel as Aersius
- Polina as Eed
- Jam as Bradwick

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

Eed returns the small chest to Hamun Kost, and relations are smoothed over. Cornelius goes to bed  
in the morning, and after a long rest, finishes deciphering the spell with the help of the Thayvian  
necromancer. Meanwhile, Eed and Aersius go on a hunting trip to replenish their food supply, and  
Bradwick tries to figure out what is up with that black dog.  
The dog remains a mystery save for the fact that it seems to follow the group and is of a corrupted  
fey nature.  
Hamun Kost mentions an expert tattooist in Thay by the name of Thunaman.  
Eed spends some time to think about her encounter with the witch.  
After wrapping up their business with Hamun Kost, the party decides to return to Phandalin by the  
Triboar trail.  
26th of Marpenoth, morning.
