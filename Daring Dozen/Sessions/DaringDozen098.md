### Summary  

The party meet at the Sleeping Giant Inn and wait for the return of Veylar. While they wait, they  
notice an old man watching Yalda especially. When confronted, he addresses her as "the one  
lightning bends towards" and tries to convince her to follow Talos in a very abrasive and  
confrontational manner that is obscured by his apparent insanity. While they talk, the party hears  
very familiar howling and the whole inn is attacked by 6 hounds of darkness. A battle ensues. During  
the battle, Yalda confronts Elvar and wants to know what he wants from her.
