# Daring Dozen — Session 69

**In-game date:** 1 Nightal afternoon to 2 Nightal early morning (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius
- Alëna as Woodruff
- Jam as Green

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

While Swift and Yalda are gone, Woodruff and Cornelius leave Green at the inn and make a stop at  
the guard station to deal with some bureaucracy. They are alerted to something suspicious going on  
there and when they enter, are attacked by a Gelatinous Cube and a drow. They manage to escape  
the drow after Cornelius was temporarily downed and retreat to the tavern where Green is waiting  
for them. The latter was trying to commune with its patron but went temporarily insane during the  
attempt. After some difficulty handling Green, they get everything under control but not without  
attracting attention. Cornelius spends some time deciphering the secret spell and some time during  
that they receive a note with the message “I miss you” from the Black Spider. They take an early  
rest. 2nd of Nightal, early morning.
