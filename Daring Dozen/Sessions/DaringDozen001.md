# Daring Dozen — Session 1

**In-game date:** Early Eleint to 14 Eleint (estimated; spans several days)

### Attending Players

- Emma as Friyola
- Moritz as Keth Thok

### Absent Players

Early sessions with Hyppolite, Friyola, and Keth Thok. Keth aided a high elf wizard Hypolite against a  
goblin ambush and the latter decided to hire the orc who agreed to the deal provided he would still  
be able to follow up on clues regarding the whereabouts of his mother. The two travelled to  
Phandalin together where they met Friyola. Among other things, they fight a manticore who is  
chased off by a white dragon and inadvertently saves the party from certain death.
