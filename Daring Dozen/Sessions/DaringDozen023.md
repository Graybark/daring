The party travel to Conyberry before the break of dawn and reach their destination late in the  
afternoon after a full day’s travel. They meet Hamun Kost, a red-robed necromancer from Thay, who  
reminds Aersius of their former master. Hamun and Cornelius make a connection over their interest  
in the arcane. Cornelius asks Hamun for help regarding his affliction and the red wizard is able to  
identify the influence of the nightwalker on Cornelius. Dio is attempting to steal from the wizard.  
24th of Marpenoth, evening.
