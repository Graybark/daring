# Daring Dozen — Session 76

**In-game date:** 4 Nightal midday to 5 Nightal late night

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Jam as Druknar Ironmind

### Absent Players

- Dimi as Cornelius

4th of Nightal, midday (2024.7.12)  
- Jam’s new (temporary) character Druknar Ironmind is introduced  
Woodruff is still feebleminded but retains some part of her consciousness with the help of the  
symbiotic mushroom that has invaded her  
- Dimi is conveniently absent this and next session and Cornelius remains feebleminded  
After a brief confrontation with Soloj, where they decide to go separate ways, the Daring Dozen loot  
the bodies and discover that one of the duergar is still alive. Druknar Ironmind joins the party after  
brief introductions. He explains the situation with the mindflayers, that they are enslaved by them  
and tells them of their village where there may be someone who could possibly heal Cornelius and  
Woodruff. Thus, they travel towards the village without much happening at first. They encounter  
places where the duergar placed runes to remember their dead since they are not allowed to keep  
their bodies. After a night’s rest, they continue to travel and when they prepare for their next night’s  
sleep, they notice rising water levels that are somehow caused by the underground river that they  
are traveling along. Druknar keeps watch but nods off and when he awakes, the water has already  
reached him and covers him almost up to his knees. He resists the effect of the strange water and  
the session ends with him alerting the others. 5th of Nightal, late night
