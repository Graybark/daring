### Summary  

We bought some new magic items. Nothing major, but Klyde got a weapon upgrade, Swift  
got a Censer of Controlling Air Elementals and we bought something to protect the whole  
group from surprise rounds. We also got some additional smaller things, but nothing crazy.  
We also still have a bunch of Gold left.  
Then we went to a meeting of underground information network people and we held a  
speech about those awful upper class people and their terrible deeds explaining the  
situation and that they should spread the information. We cast Mass Suggestion on 8  
people and it worked on 4 of them.  
Then we had time left in the day and teleported to the location where the fungal infection is  
happening thinking to investigate what's going on there and return to the city the next day  
for the second day of creating civil unrest. We missed the mark by a few miles on the  
teleport and were falling from the sky. Feather Fall gave us time to decide what to do and  
Woodruff and Swift (thanks to Polymorph) turned into giant eagles and carried the rest of us  
to the target location, avoiding half a day of travel through the forest by one hour of flight  
time. At the target location we located a clearing that we landed on. On that clearing  
Woodruff reached out using her fungal feelers to see if she can locate this other fungal  
network. She could and reached out to it. Only to be slapped by 37 damage as it lashed out  
at her immediately. She was however able to tell that the source (or should we say part of  
the source) was attached to the roots of some trees in the middle of this clearing. Analyzing  
those trees together with the help of Bridds they soon realized that what looked like trees  
were not trees at all. But instead something else, with a different biological make up and  
also very much moving, pretending to be trees. During this realization Swift became more  
and more aware that this entire place didn't feel quite "right". Things at the edge of the  
clearing seemed to move ever so slightly in the corner of their vision.  
Bridds, being very curious about this strange creature in front of them and having seen  
Woodruff reach out to its consciousness attempted then to also reach out to this being with  
his own telepathic capability and succeeded. This caused things to escalate. The floor began  
to move almost as though there was a layer of water beneath the grass. And when Bridd~s  
attempted to ask this being a question it lashed out physically, as an enormous Vine-like  
tentacles burst out from the ground and slapped Bridd~s with some bludgeoning and  
necrotic damage twice. We are now at the start of the combat encounter, with the first  
enemy having made its attacks on Bridds.
