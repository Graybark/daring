5th of Nightal, late night (2024.7.26)  
Woodruff encounters the deep dragon who is entirely subsumed by the fungal network. The dragon  
is typically evil (nature check) and delights in knowledge of distant places. This particular dragon  
however, entered, voluntarily or not, into a symbiotic relationship with some species of fungus that  
has shifted the dragon’s alignment to neutral. This new creature’s goal is to spread as much as  
possible, even to places outside of the Underdark. Everything the fungal network experiences, the  
dragon also experiences and benefits from thusly. There are multiple strains of this (nature check)  
and some are nigh indetectable, and there also seems to be a faint trace of magic within the fungus  
(arcana check).  
The party need to find a way to get into the duergar village without catching the attention of the  
illithid – the entrance is by the githyanki grave  
Assuming the party takes a long rest after the fight, it is now early morning, 6th of Nightal.  
Woodruff speaks to the dragon and enters some kind of symbiotic relationship with it. She agrees to  
spread the fungus network on the surface so the dragon-fungus symbiote may discover that world  
too. She manages to rejoin the group just in time to hide with them from the approaching  
mindflayer patrol. They find themselves in the duergar mines waiting for the patrol to pass.  
Mindflayer Influence:  
Dark, twisting tendrils of mindflayer influence can be seen throughout the village. Mindflayer guards  
occasionally patrol the area, their presence a constant reminder of the duergar’s enslavement. The  
villagers move with a wary vigilance, their every action shadowed by the oppressive control of their  
psionic masters.  
The village shaman and healer, Thalgrim is an elderly duergar with a long, braided white beard and  
piercing gray eyes. He carries a staff adorned with all manner of glowing gems and secretly practices  
ancient healing arts to aid his people. Under the oppressive rule of the mindflayers, Thalgrim serves  
as a beacon of hope and resilience, discreetly offering spiritual guidance and healing to those in  
need.  
The village blacksmith, Dargol is a burly duergar with arms like tree trunks and a face perpetually  
smudged with soot. Forced to craft weapons and armor for the mindflayers, he secretly forges  
hidden compartments and weak points into his creations to sabotage their efforts.  
The chief miner, Morgra is a middle-aged duergar woman with sharp features and a no-nonsense  
attitude. She oversees the village's mining operations, extracting valuable resources for the  
mindflayers. Despite the heavy surveillance, Morgra organizes secret escape routes and hidden  
caches of supplies, preparing for the day they can revolt.  
The village scout and hunter, Beldrak is lean and agile, with a knack for moving silently through the  
tunnels. He’s forced to track and capture cave creatures for the mindflayers' experiments. Beldrak  
secretly gathers intelligence on the mindflayers' movements and weaknesses, sharing his findings  
with the resistance.  
The village brewer, Gritta is a jovial duergar with a round face and a hearty laugh. Her tavern has  
become a haven for secret meetings among the villagers. She brews a variety of ales and spirits that  
are the pride of the village, often using her gatherings to spread hope and unity among her  
oppressed kin.  
The village leader, Kragar is a stoic and resolute duergar with a scarred face and piercing blue eyes.  
Once a proud warrior, he now leads his people under the watchful eyes of the mindflayers. Kragar  
maintains a facade of compliance while secretly coordinating the village's resistance efforts.
