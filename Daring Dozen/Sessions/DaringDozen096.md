### Summary  

Woodruff loses her concentration and her draconic transformation vanishes. The party struggled  
against the hivemind and while they subdued it with the help of Onyx, Bridds was grafted into a  
hulk, his body destroyed. They only found the remnants of Bridds's finger preserved inside a glass  
vial. They harvest unique parts of the hivemind for evidence and take a long rest inside a Tiny Hut.  
While they were sleeping, the trees moved around the hut and when they wake up they found  
themselves encircled. Woodruff communicated with them via the Speak with Plants spell and found  
out that they were fighting against Onyx's influence. They rolled CON saves and Woodruff, Klyde,  
and Swift failed, the spores taking root inside their lungs just before they teleport in front of the city  
gates of Neverwinter.
