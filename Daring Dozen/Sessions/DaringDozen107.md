### Summary  

The party tried to find Veylar to dischuss his dragon hoard but were not able to find him. They then  
decided to locate the Black Spider to collect their reward but were unable to find him in his lair. He  
still left them 4000 gold and considered the debt settled because of the costs incurred. Since Klyde  
and Yalda also entered the Wave Echo Cave, they got a free feat from the magic-infused  
atmosphere. After they got a letter of recommentation from Varos, they teleported to Waterdeep to  
talk to a priest of Selune there who informed them that the city of Waterdeep is besieged by storm  
and undead. This is where they met Ithrelle who is also on a quest to banish the Nightwalker. The  
four make their way to the northern city walls where they hope to find more information and maybe  
help the soldiers. On their way, they get enveloped by magical mist and initiate combat against a  
vampire who has been stalking them using animals. They fight it and after some struggle manage to  
wound it, after which Yalda threatens her which gives the vampire pause.
