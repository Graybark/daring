# Daring Dozen — Session 91 — Cedric's Support

**In-game date:** 12 Nightal night to 13 Nightal early morning (end estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Dimi as Cornelius

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

12th of Nightal, night  
The party seeks out Cedric Thornton, brother to Mariel Thornton, to begin their quest to convince  
the council to seek more peaceful and sustainable means of logging. After they manage to enter the  
veritable fortress that is Cedric's mansion, they are told that only one person is allowed to speak to  
Cedric. Cornelius is chosen and the two bond over Cedric's fascination with bridges. Cornelius even  
offers to cast the simulacrum spell for Cedric to carve out more time for the latter's passion.  
To Cedric, the deaths are inconvenient and a bad look so he suggests that the party gathers some  
evidence of what is happening in the forest first before they can get his full support and before they  
seek out the other council members.
