12th of Nightal, early evening (2025.1.17)  
The party meets Veylar, who, under the alias of Veylar Ashbough, claims to be negotiating with the  
Neverwinter Council to implement sustainable deforestation. He casts Modify Memory on  
Woodruff, making her believe she has always known him as the protector of her village. Yalda  
remains suspicious, nearly convincing Woodruff to cast Greater Restoration to reveal the truth.
