### Summary

Yalda negotiated with the Listener. She agreed to trap the aberration for him, with forfeiture of her soul after death specified as a punishment, and in exchange the Listener agreed to kill the Pale Child for the party. Yalda signed the contract using another person's name and believed she had tricked the Listener. Whether that signature changes the contract's power or validity is **To confirm**; the Listener's promised killing has not yet happened.

Yalda then reunited with the party in front of the Horned Regent's palace. After negotiating with a guard, they entered and noticed approximately half a dozen strikingly lifelike statues that resembled the party and appeared to be petrified people.

The party was introduced to the Horned Regent, Morthakar. They negotiated an agreement to deal with the Nightwalker, which had taken control of the city's machinery. In return, they asked only for equipment. They took supplies including three vials of Basilisk Oil, potions, and numerous magical weapons of up to +2. The regent also gave them permission to free Ivandra, a planewalker who had been petrified, so she could aid them in the fight. Whether she was restored during the session is **To confirm**.

The party managed to short-circuit the control room from outside and gain entry. Inside they found the Nightwalker manipulating the sphere's engine; it initially appeared not to notice them. They positioned themselves before the Nightwalker became aware of them. Initiative was rolled, and the session ended **before combat began**.
