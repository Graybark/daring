# Daring Dozen — Session 13

**In-game date:** 25 Eleint morning to 28 Eleint noon (start estimated)

## Archived notes

> These archived notes include GM preparation. They do not establish that every described scene happened in play.

### Attending Players

- Márton as Hyppolite
- Dimi as Cornelius
- Jam as Bradwick
- Mahmoud as Dio

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

They travel back to Phandalin. Found a young dire wolf on the way. Cornelius attempted to make a  
looking glass and nearly succeeded. Hyppolite and Bradwick bond a little bit over their fitness  
training. They meet Halia Thornton and talk to Sildar about Gundren and his possible demise. Dio  
and Bradwick entertain. They get invited by Halia to visit her at the Miner’s Exchange and are on  
their way there when the session ends. 28th of Eleint, noon. Expenses paid.
