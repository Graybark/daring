### Summary  

The party's ship was attacked by a larger vessel manned by Talos cultists. After a quick fight where  
Sickening Radiance cast by Cornelius made short work of most of the pirates, they discovered that  
Vaeril's soul was somehow attached to this ship and he was controlling it this whole time, that he  
was the ship itself. They convince him that he should let go of his anger and accept that he made  
mistakes and that they would help him be accepted by Talos in turn. He agreed to help them get to  
the island.
