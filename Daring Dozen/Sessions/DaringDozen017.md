# Daring Dozen — Session 17

**In-game date:** 28 Eleint early evening to late evening (start estimated)

## Archived notes

### Attending Players

- Rachel as Aersius
- Monika as Hope

### Absent Players

- Not recorded.

Aersius and Hope meet at the tavern and decide to join forces to investigate the Redbrand base  
because Aersius has a grudge against the gang since they beat her senseless and left her to die in the  
streets. Hope is investigating a lead of the presence of kobolds in the Neverwinter Woods and  
looking for reinforcement. The two find the secret cave entrance to the Redbrand headquarters and  
encounter a mysterious creature there who speaks to them telepathically and can deal necrotic  
damage. They run from it and meet the rest of the party. The entire party is ambushed by 5  
Redbrand thugs but dispatch them with ease. There is major disagreement on how to handle the last  
of the thugs who surrendered. Bradwick defends his life, but the rest disagrees with his idea of  
mercy and manage to kill him in the end. 28th of Eleint, late evening.
