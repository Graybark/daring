### Summary  

The party defeat the Hounds of Darkness but let one escape. Elvar Kurn manages to sneak away.  
They save most of the inn guests and contact Lady Mariel Thornton and manage to give her most of  
the credit by pretending that they acted in her name. They travel with her to her offices and discover  
on their way that Veylar has been magically controlling her mind. They free her of the Geas spell and  
now fear that Veylar noticed that while sitting atop their carriage with Woodruff. They are afraid of  
being held hostage now and have warned Lord Neverember to prepare for a magical attack.
