5th of Nightal, late night (2024.7.19)  
The party is suspicious of the water, and they try to escape its encroaching by traveling along the  
river in the hopes of evading it by speed alone. When Klyde casts Fly on himself, he is suddenly  
attacked by a spout of water that almost knocks him out of the air. They roll initiative and fight 4  
large sea demons. There are some tense moments when Klyde and Woodruff are both in danger but  
they manage to send the demons back to their home plane without incurring any losses. Woodruff  
however, manages to transform into an eagle using her druid powers and leaves the party, ever  
drawn by that mysterious entity in her head.
