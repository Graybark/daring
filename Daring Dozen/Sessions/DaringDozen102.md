### Summary  

Yalda, Klyde, and Bridds engaged with the drow when they were ambushed by 4 other drow who  
were hiding behind a cover of magical darkness. Woodruff dispelled the darkness by a powerful spell  
and made them easier targets for the group who killed the initial attacker who is trying to escape.  
They managed to bring down Yalda and picked Bridds as their next target. Unfortunately for the  
enemy, Bridds is immune to their necrotic blades. This discouraged them and they fled but not  
before Bridds sicced a ghost on them who is able to track them for an hour. Bridds tracked them to a  
run-down building where they seem to recover from their wounds. Bridds remembers this location.  
After this night the party rested and when they woke up, Klyde and Veylar were gone. They tracked  
them down in the Shard of the Moon where they witnessed a much weakened Veylar being treated  
by Varos with the assistance of Klyde. Veylar told them that he was slowly dying while he was  
essentially holding back the influence of this infecting power which has been trying to get into this  
world for over a hundred years but has increased its efforts during the last year or so.  
Swift is now also chased by the Tea Party.
