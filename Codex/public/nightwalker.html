Traits

Annihilating Aura. Enemies in a magical 30-foot Emanation around the Nightwalker can’t regain Hit Points. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 26, each enemy that starts its turn there. Failure: 22 (4d10) Necrotic damage.

Life Eater. A creature dies if reduced to 0 Hit Points by the Nightwalker and can’t be revived except by a Wish spell.

Overwhelming Necrosis. The Nightwalker’s Necrotic damage ignores Resistance to Necrotic damage.

Legendary Resistance (4/Day). If the Nightwalker fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

Magic Resistance. The Nightwalker has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

Incorporeal Movement. The Nightwalker can move through creatures and objects as Difficult Terrain. It takes 22 (4d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

Far Realm Unbinding (1/Day). When the Nightwalker would first drop to 0 Hit Points or be killed outright, it transforms into the Unmoored Anchor instead. See The Anchor Opens. No action is required, even while Incapacitated.

Actions

Multiattack. The Nightwalker makes three Entropic Rend attacks.

Entropic Rend. Melee Attack Roll: +19, reach 20 ft. Hit: 28 (4d8 + 10) Bludgeoning damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage. Once on each of its own turns, the Nightwalker can also reduce the target’s HP maximum by the Necrotic damage taken, to a minimum of 1.

Oblivion Wave (Recharge 5–6). Constitution Saving Throw: DC 26, each creature in a 120-foot Cone. Failure: 78 (12d12) Necrotic damage. Success: Half damage.

Bonus Actions

Dreadful Gaze. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 26, one creature the Nightwalker can see within 120 feet. Failure: The target is Frightened until the end of the Nightwalker’s next turn.

Step Through Shadow. The Nightwalker magically teleports up to 90 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

Legendary Actions

Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature’s turn, the Nightwalker can spend one use on one option below. It regains all uses at the start of its turn and can’t use them while Incapacitated.

Entropic Rend. The Nightwalker makes one Entropic Rend attack.

Deathly Passage. The Nightwalker magically teleports up to 90 feet to an unoccupied space it can see and makes one Entropic Rend attack.

Grasp of the Void. Strength Saving Throw: DC 26, one creature the Nightwalker can see within 90 feet. Failure: 45 (10d8) Necrotic damage, and the Nightwalker can pull the target straight toward itself along a clear path. Success: Half damage only. It can’t use this option again until the start of its next turn.

Phase I — Lair Actions

Silence of the Grave. A 60-foot-radius Sphere anywhere in the lair falls silent until the next Initiative 20. Creatures inside are Deafened and can’t cast spells with Verbal components. A creature concentrating on a spell when the silence appears must succeed on a DC 26 Constitution saving throw or lose Concentration.

Iron Lockdown. One vault doorway or bridge anywhere in the lair seals behind heavy bulkheads, blocking passage and providing Total Cover until the next Initiative 20.

Light's Last Breath. All nonmagical light sources in the lair are extinguished, and magical light created by spells of level 5 or lower is suppressed until the next Initiative 20. The Nightwalker can see normally.

Phase II — Lair Actions

Temporal Drag. A 30-foot-radius Sphere centered anywhere in the lair slows time until the next Initiative 20. A creature in the area has its Speed halved and can take only one of an Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction each round. The restriction ends upon leaving the area.

Contradictory Gravity. Until the next Initiative 20, gravity abruptly returns throughout the vault, pulling every creature and unsecured object toward a surface the Anchor chooses. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 24, each creature not anchored to a surface. Failure: The creature is hurled up to 30 feet toward that surface and falls Prone upon impact. Success: The creature grabs a handhold and remains in place. The Anchor is unaffected.

Uncertain Futures. Until the next Initiative 20, enemies in the lair can’t benefit from Advantage on attack rolls or saving throws. The Anchor is unaffected.

Death Heart

Transformation, lair actions, and devices — outside the stat blocks

The Anchor Opens

Replace Phase I with Phase II at 350 HP, applying ongoing maximum-HP reductions; a maximum of 0 means defeat. This is a form change, not healing. Excess damage from the triggering hit is lost; subsequent attacks hit Phase II. Its aura ends, but inflicted maximum-HP reductions remain.

Phase II receives four fresh Legendary Resistances and Memory Collapse ready. Other ongoing effects, Initiative, activated devices, and spent actions persist unless their rules say otherwise. No extra turn, attack, movement, damage burst, or refreshed Legendary Actions. Rifts begin on its next normal turn.

Vault Rules

Three connected, gravityless chambers; handrails, broad openings, and Total Cover. Place the Nexus centrally and the other devices 40–60 feet away. Keep an exit beyond the tether.

Tether. Beyond 240 feet from the Nexus, the Anchor’s rifts end and Spatial Fractures, Fold Space, and Impossible Passage are unavailable. Inside that radius, their rift centers and teleport destinations must also stay inside it. Measure from the center of the Anchor’s space.

Movement. Handrails permit walking-Speed travel. Teleport destinations must be visible to their chooser, unoccupied, and fit the target; hazards and unsupported spaces remain allowed. Without a legal destination, omit the teleport. Rifts trigger only on the affected creature’s own turn.

Devices & Sealing

Within 5 feet, spend one Action to activate a device. No check. It stays active for the encounter, across both phases.

Ward RegulatorPhase I: suppresses Annihilating Aura. Phase II: the party and its allies take half Psychic damage within 30 feet of the Regulator. This is not Resistance. Life Eater remains active.

Seal. Once all three devices are active and Phase II is Bloodied (175 HP or fewer) within its tether, a creature within 5 feet of the Nexus spends one Action to seal the manifestation and win. No second operator, check, or countdown.

Reducing Phase II to 0 HP also wins. The greater entity’s fate and the Nightwalker’s survival remain To confirm.

GM notes & continuity

Lethality. Phase I’s Life Eater remains active; lair actions deal no damage. Death Ward resolves before reaching 0 HP. Necrotic/Psychic resistance bypass applies only to matching damage, not these lair actions. Phase II has no Life Eater.

Environment. Keep the Negative Plane house rules: in Death Heart, 1d6 Necrotic damage per minute without Death Ward, none with it. This environmental drain does not trigger Life Eater. Breathing protection remains necessary.

Balance. Untested: 550/350 HP, four independent resistances per phase. Lair actions alter terrain and tactics without extra damage; permanent devices make sealing easier. Creature statistics are unchanged from the published revision 2.5.
