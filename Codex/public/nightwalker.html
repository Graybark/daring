Annihilating Aura. Enemies in a magical 30-foot Emanation around the Nightwalker can’t regain Hit Points. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 26, each enemy that starts its turn there. Failure: 22 (4d10) Necrotic damage.

Life Eater. A creature dies if reduced to 0 Hit Points by the Nightwalker and can’t be revived except by a Wish spell.

Legendary Resistance (4/Day). If the Nightwalker fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

Magic Resistance. The Nightwalker has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

Incorporeal Movement. The Nightwalker can move through creatures and objects as Difficult Terrain. It takes 22 (4d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

Far Realm Unbinding (1/Day). When the Nightwalker would first drop to 0 Hit Points or be killed outright, it transforms into the Unmoored Anchor instead. See The Anchor Opens. No action is required, even while Incapacitated.

Transformation and lair effects · separate from the creature stat blocks

The Anchor Opens

Replace Phase I with Phase II. Its HP maximum and current HP become 350, minus any ongoing maximum-HP reductions. If that leaves a maximum of 0 or less, it is defeated. This is a form change, not healing; excess damage from the triggering hit doesn't carry over.

Phase II starts with four fresh Legendary Resistances and Memory Collapse available. Unused Phase I resistances are discarded. Initiative, remaining Legendary Action uses, other ongoing effects, and machinery progress persist unless an effect's rules make it inapplicable. The Phase I aura ends; inflicted maximum-HP reductions remain.

The transformation grants no extra turn, action, movement, or damage burst and refreshes no spent actions or Reactions. Later attacks in the triggering action resolve against Phase II. Fractures first appear at the start of the Anchor’s next normal turn. There is no third phase.

Death Heart: Lair Effects and Controls

Use three linked maintenance chambers with broad openings, shafts, handrails, and Total Cover. Place the fixed Nexus centrally, with the Regulator and Conduit about 40–60 feet away on different approaches. Keep an exit beyond the 120-foot tether. This remains Death Heart's crowded, gravityless interior, not an empty sphere.

Bound Manifestation (lair effect). While the center of the Anchor’s space is more than 120 feet from the fixed Nexus, its fractures end and it can’t use Impossible Causality, Fold Space, or Impossible Passage. Within the tether, its fracture centers and destinations for Fold Space and Impossible Passage must remain within 120 feet of the Nexus. The controls below modify these abilities further. These are arena rules, not additional creature traits or initiative-count lair actions.

Movement. Handrails allow travel at walking Speed without flight. Open-space movement follows the campaign's gravityless rules (To confirm where unspecified). Every teleport destination must accommodate the creature. Hostile teleports must stay inside the vault, adjacent to a handhold unless the target can fly, and cannot cause immediate damage on arrival. If no legal destination exists, omit the teleport. Entering a fracture on another creature’s turn causes no hazard save.

Operate a control

Within 5 feet of a device, use an Action to attempt a DC 23 Strength (Athletics), Dexterity using Thieves' Tools, or Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) check. A success stabilizes it. After a failure, the next Action spent operating that device stabilizes it automatically, even by another creature. Stabilization persists through both phases.

Explain each device's function before combat; discovering the basic controls needs no check.

Until the end of the boss's next turn: in Phase I, suppress Annihilating Aura; in Phase II, creatures chosen by the operator have Resistance to Psychic damage while within 30 feet of the Regulator. Repeating a pulse refreshes its duration, not its strength. Pulses don't suppress Life Eater or repeat automatically on transformation.

Seal the manifestation

With all three devices stabilized and the Phase II Anchor Bloodied and within its tether, a creature at the Nexus can use an Action to begin sealing. A different creature at any control must spend an Action to finish before the Anchor’s next turn, while the requirements still hold. No checks or Concentration are required. Completion defeats the local manifestation; a missed window can be retried without losing stabilization.

Alternatively, defeat Phase II by reducing it to 0 HP. Neither outcome automatically destroys the greater aberration or guarantees the Nightwalker’s survival.

GM notes, continuity, and read-aloud

Life Eater is always active in Phase I. It applies whenever that form reduces a creature to 0 HP, regardless of damage type. Only Wish can revive the victim. Effects such as Death Ward that prevent reaching 0 HP resolve first. Phase II has no Life Eater. Make the lethal stakes known before combat.

Environment. Keep the Negative Plane house rules: Death Heart drains 1d6 Necrotic damage per minute without Death Ward, and none with it. Preserve that timer through combat. Environmental damage isn't the Nightwalker’s damage and doesn't trigger Life Eater. Death Ward doesn't grant general protection from the monster's aura. Breathing protection remains necessary. Entropic Rend's maximum-HP reduction and the environment's reduction retain their separate recovery rules.

Tactics. In Phase I, choose between frightening a target and teleporting with the Bonus Action; pull enemies toward the aura with Grasp. In Phase II, use fractures and tendrils to reposition combatants. Once the Conduit is stabilized, let the players exploit the windows. When the Anchor becomes Bloodied, reveal the sealing opportunity.

Rules. These magical monster abilities aren't spells. Apply normal cover, damage resistance, teleportation prevention, and condition rules. Truesight doesn't see through walls. Neither phase has additional unlisted reactions, immunities, or lair actions.

Difficulty. The baseline has 900 nominal HP and four independent Legendary Resistances per phase. Attack-only cycles of three normal attacks and three Legendary Action attacks deal 252/264 damage if all hit, before aura or fracture damage; this is not an expected-DPR calculation. Life Eater applies regardless of damage type and permits revival only through Wish. Both phases are immune to Grappled, Petrified, and Restrained. Balance remains untested.

For a softer test, use 450/250 HP, two Legendary Action uses, and DCs 24/22 for Phases I/II; retain four resistances per phase. Current party readiness: To confirm. Use campaign advancement; an optional 310,000 XP award covers the entire encounter or successful seal, not each phase (To confirm).

Track: HP; current phase's four resistances; three Legendary Action uses and once-until-next-turn options; one recharge; fear/reaction-denial durations; three controls; any ward pulse; two fracture tokens; any pending seal. No marks or psychic-echo tokens are used.

Player-Facing Read-Aloud — Future Scenes Only

First sight.

Between broken conduits stands an enormous darkness, its hands pressed against the machinery. Behind its ribs, another shape moves a moment later than it should.

Transformation.

The shape tears apart, but nothing falls. The walls behind it turn, revealing a room that cannot be there. Your voices return from the opening, speaking words you have not said.

Exposed connection.

The three machines fall into the same rhythm. Threads of darkness draw tight around a single point. The thing inside the silhouette seems unable to decide where it is.
