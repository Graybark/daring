Traits

Annihilating Aura. Enemies in a magical 30-foot Emanation around the Nightwalker can’t regain Hit Points. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 26, each enemy that starts its turn there. Failure: 22 (4d10) Necrotic damage.

Life Eater. A creature dies if reduced to 0 Hit Points by the Nightwalker and can’t be revived except by a Wish spell.

Overwhelming Necrosis. The Nightwalker’s Necrotic damage ignores Resistance to Necrotic damage.

Soul Reaping. Once per turn, when the Nightwalker deals Necrotic damage to a creature, it regains Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt to that one creature, up to its Hit Point maximum.

Legendary Resistance (4/Day). If the Nightwalker fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

Magic Resistance. The Nightwalker has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

Incorporeal Movement. The Nightwalker can move through creatures and objects as Difficult Terrain. It takes 22 (4d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

Far Realm Unbinding (1/Day). When the Nightwalker would first drop to 0 Hit Points or be killed outright, it transforms into the Unmoored Anchor instead. See The Anchor Opens. No action is required, even while Incapacitated.

Actions

Multiattack. The Nightwalker makes three Entropic Rend attacks.

Entropic Rend. Melee Attack Roll: +19, reach 20 ft. Hit: 56 (8d8 + 20) Force damage plus 28 (8d6) Necrotic damage. Once on each of its own turns, the Nightwalker can also reduce the target’s HP maximum by the Necrotic damage taken, to a minimum of 1.

Oblivion Wave (Recharge 5–6). Constitution Saving Throw: DC 26, each other creature in a 120-foot-radius Sphere centered on the Nightwalker. Failure: 78 (12d12) Necrotic damage. Success: Half damage.

Spellcasting. The Nightwalker casts the following spells without components (spell save DC 26): 1/Day Each: Power Word Kill, Mass Suggestion, Dominate Monster, Antimagic Field.

Bonus Actions

Dreadful Gaze. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 26, one creature the Nightwalker can see within 120 feet. Failure: The target is Frightened until the end of the Nightwalker’s next turn.

Step Through Shadow. The Nightwalker magically teleports up to 90 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

Reactions

Reaction Uses: 2 per round. Regains both at the start of its turn; only one Reaction per trigger.

Counterspell. The Nightwalker casts Counterspell without components.

Shield. The Nightwalker casts Shield without components.

Legendary Actions

Legendary Action Uses: 4 (minus 1 per active device).

Entropic Rend. The Nightwalker makes one Entropic Rend attack.

Deathly Passage. The Nightwalker magically teleports up to 90 feet to an unoccupied space it can see and makes one Entropic Rend attack.

Grasp of the Void. Strength Saving Throw: DC 26, one creature the Nightwalker can see within 90 feet. Failure: 90 (20d8) Necrotic damage, and the Nightwalker can pull the target straight toward itself along a clear path. Success: Half damage only. It can’t use this option again until the start of its next turn.

Phase I — Lair Actions

Silence of the Grave. The entire lair falls silent until the next Initiative 20. Creatures in the lair are Deafened and can’t cast spells with Verbal components. A creature concentrating on a spell when the silence appears must succeed on a DC 26 Constitution saving throw or lose Concentration.

Iron Lockdown. Every vault doorway and bridge seals behind heavy bulkheads, blocking passage and providing Total Cover until the next Initiative 20.

Death Surge. The Nightwalker floods all chambers with necrotic energy. Each chamber's machine loses one stage of progress (minimum Stage 0). Each other creature in the lair must make a DC 26 Constitution saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one.

Overwhelming Psionics. The Anchor’s Psychic damage ignores Resistance to Psychic damage.

Spatial Fractures. At the start of its turn, the Anchor twists space around up to three enemies it can see within 120 feet. Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 25, each target. Failure: 22 (4d10) Force damage, and the Anchor teleports the target up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see. Success: Half damage and no teleport.

Actions

Multiattack. The Anchor makes three Unmaking Tendril attacks.

Unmaking Tendril. Ranged Attack Roll: +17, range 120 ft. Hit: The target makes a DC 25 Intelligence saving throw. Failure: 44 (8d8 + 8) Force damage plus 30 (5d8 + 8) Psychic damage, and all magical effects currently affecting the target end immediately, including spells and magical conditions. Success: Force damage only.

Memory Collapse (Recharge 5–6). Intelligence Saving Throw: DC 25, each enemy in a 60-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the Anchor can see within 120 feet. Failure: 54 (12d8) Psychic damage, and the target loses Concentration (if any). Until the end of its next turn, it can’t take Reactions and has Disadvantage on attack rolls. Success: Half damage only.

Spellcasting. The Anchor uses Intelligence as its spellcasting ability and casts the following spells without components (spell save DC 25): 1/Day Each: Psychic Scream, Maze, Reverse Gravity, Reality Break, Forcecage. 2/Day Each: Synaptic Static, Telekinesis, Wall of Force.

Gravity Hook. Strength Saving Throw: DC 25, one creature the Anchor can see within 120 feet. Failure: Pulled up to 60 feet straight toward the Anchor along an unobstructed path; Speed 0 until the start of the Anchor’s next turn.

Reactions

Reaction Uses: 2 per round. Regains both at the start of its turn; only one Reaction per trigger.

Temporal Shunt. The Anchor casts Temporal Shunt without components (spell save DC 25).

Silvery Barbs. The Anchor casts Silvery Barbs without components.

Spellcasting (Costs 1 Legendary Action). The Anchor casts one spell from its Spellcasting trait, expending one of that spell’s daily uses.

Sabotage. One device anywhere in the lair loses one step of progress.

Spatial Implosion. Space folds inward around one creature anywhere in the lair, crushing it from all directions before snapping back. This effect can target a creature behind Total Cover or in another chamber. Constitution Saving Throw: DC 25, that creature. Failure: 72 (16d8) Force damage. Success: Half damage.

Phase II — Lair Actions

Temporal Drag. Time slows throughout the lair until the next Initiative 20. Each enemy in the lair has its Speed halved and can take only one of an Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction each round.

Fractured Coordinates. The chambers fold into impossible positions. Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 25, each enemy in the lair. Failure: The Anchor teleports the creature to an unoccupied space in a different chamber. Until the next Initiative 20, it can’t leave that chamber by any means; passages lead back inside. Success: No effect.

Uncertain Futures. Until the next Initiative 20, enemies in the lair can’t benefit from Advantage on any D20 Test (ability checks, attack rolls, or saving throws). The Anchor is unaffected.

Death Heart

Transformation, lair actions, and devices — outside the stat blocks

The Anchor Opens

Replace Phase I with Phase II at 350 HP, applying ongoing maximum-HP reductions; a maximum of 0 means defeat. This is a form change, not healing. Excess damage from the triggering hit is lost; subsequent attacks hit Phase II. Its aura ends, but inflicted maximum-HP reductions remain.

Phase II receives four fresh Legendary Resistances and Memory Collapse ready. Other ongoing effects, Initiative, activated devices, and spent actions persist unless their rules say otherwise. No extra turn, attack, movement, damage burst, or refreshed Legendary Actions. Spatial Fractures resumes on its next normal turn.

Vault Rules

Three connected chambers with broad openings, raised walkways, machinery, and Total Cover. Place the Nexus centrally and the other devices 40–60 feet away. Keep an exit beyond the tether.

Tether. Beyond 240 feet from the Nexus, the Anchor’s rifts end and Spatial Fractures, Fold Space, and Impossible Passage are unavailable. Inside that radius, their teleport destinations must also stay inside it. Measure from the center of the Anchor’s space.

Movement. Teleport destinations must be visible to their chooser, unoccupied, and fit the target; hazards remain allowed. Without a legal destination, omit the teleport.

Devices & Sealing

Two steps per device. A creature within 5 feet spends one Action to prime a device, then another Action to activate it. No checks. Different creatures can perform the steps on different turns. Progress persists across both boss phases.

Shared effect — all three devices. Only a fully activated device reduces the boss’s Legendary Action uses by 1 in both phases (4 → 3 → 2 → 1). Priming alone has no effect. The reduction applies immediately and persists through the transformation.

Devices: Ward Regulator · Spatial Conduit · Anchor Nexus.

Seal. Once all three devices are active and Phase II is Bloodied (175 HP or fewer) within its tether, a creature within 5 feet of the Nexus spends one Action to seal the manifestation and win.

Reducing Phase II to 0 HP also wins. The greater entity’s fate and the Nightwalker’s survival remain To confirm.

GM notes & continuity

Lethality. Phase I’s Life Eater remains active; most lair actions deal no damage. Death Ward resolves before reaching 0 HP. Necrotic/Psychic resistance bypass applies only to matching damage, not these lair actions. Phase II has no Life Eater.

Environment. Keep the Negative Plane house rules: in Death Heart, 1d6 Necrotic damage per minute without Death Ward, none with it. This environmental drain does not trigger Life Eater. Breathing protection remains necessary.

Balance. Untested: 550/350 HP, four independent resistances per phase. Lair actions alter terrain and tactics without extra damage; active devices reduce Legendary Action uses and enable sealing. Creature statistics are unchanged from the published revision 2.5.

Track: HP, four resistances, Legendary Action uses (4 minus active devices), recharge, conditions, last lair action, six device-step checkboxes.

Continuity. The Death Heart preparation supplies the vault and Shar’s Favored. The played Session 132 record confirms the tuning fork’s creation, not its powers.

To confirm: the aberration’s identity, Shar’s possible betrayal, the Nightwalker’s agency and survival, machinery and fork powers, party readiness, planar origin, Entropic Rend’s recovery duration, and Grasp of the Void’s pull limit.
